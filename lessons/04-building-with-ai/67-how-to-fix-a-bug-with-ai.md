# How to Fix a Bug with AI

> ⏱ 2 min read · 🟡 Intermediate

## One Line Answer
When something breaks in your code, you can paste the problem into an AI tool and ask it to explain and fix the issue for you.

## Real World Analogy
It's like texting a mechanic a photo of your check engine light — you don't need to know what's wrong yourself, you just need to describe what you're seeing and let the expert diagnose it.

## Live Example
Go to [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com) or [Claude](https://claude.ai) and paste in a broken piece of HTML or CSS. Tell it "this isn't working and I don't know why." It will explain the problem in plain English and show you a corrected version.

## How It Shows Up in a Real App

Say your button isn't showing up on the page. You might have this:

```html
<buton>Click Me</buton>
```

You paste that into an AI tool and say: *"My button isn't appearing correctly."* The AI spots the typo (`buton` instead of `button`), explains why it matters, and gives you the fixed version:

```html
<button>Click Me</button>
```

No Googling, no guessing — you get an answer and understand why it was wrong.

## What Breaks Without It
Without a way to diagnose bugs quickly, small typos or missing pieces can stall your entire project for hours. New developers often can't spot their own mistakes because they don't yet know what "correct" is supposed to look like.

## What to Tell AI When You Need It

> "Here's my code: [paste code]. It's supposed to [describe what it should do] but instead [describe what's actually happening]. What's wrong and how do I fix it?"

> "I'm a beginner learning HTML and CSS. Can you explain this bug in simple terms and show me the corrected code with a short explanation of what went wrong?"

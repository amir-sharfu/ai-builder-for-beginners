# What is the Internet?

> ⏱ 2 min read · 🟢 Beginner

## One Line Answer
The internet is a giant network of computers around the world that can send information to each other.

## Real World Analogy
Think of the internet like a postal system — but instead of letters and packages, it carries digital information (text, images, videos, messages). Every computer connected to the internet has an address, just like your home has a postal address.

## Live Example
Open **https://www.google.com**

That page loaded because:
1. Your computer sent a request through the internet
2. Google's computer (server) received it
3. Google sent the webpage back to you
4. Your browser displayed it

All of that happened in under a second.

## How It Shows Up in Our App
When you type a message in our chat app and hit send — that message travels over the internet from your browser to our server, gets processed by the AI, and the response travels back to you over the internet.

## What Breaks Without It
Everything. No internet = no app. The frontend and backend cannot talk to each other. The AI API cannot be reached.

## What to Tell AI When You Need It
> "My app needs to work online and send data between a browser and a server. Set it up so both can communicate over the internet."
